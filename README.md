5. Design and User Experience 

Overall design strategy: clean, contemporary, polished, and inspired by nature, with a focus on trust and readability. 

The suggested colour scheme consists of white, light green, dark green, and a warm accent colour. Green will convey growth, sustainability, and agriculture. 

Typography: Use a clear sans-serif typeface, such Arial, Inter, or another Google or web-safe font. Headings and body text will be visibly different. 

Layout and design: a wide opening section, a structured footer, cards for services and projects, a consistent header and navigation bar, and well divided content sections. 

Simple navigation, readable writing, sufficient contrast, informative links, responsive design, accessible form labels, and obvious calls to action are all important aspects of user experience.
