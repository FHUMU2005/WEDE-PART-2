About Kunyarungo Agritech Solutions

We are a South African agricultural technology company that helps small-scale farmers adopt useful, sustainable technology. We focus on solar-powered irrigation, smart farming and efficient water management.

Our team works on the farm with you, from the first site visit to training. See everything we offer on our services page.

To use affordable, sustainable and useful agricultural technology to help small-scale farmers increase production.

Our Vision

Technologically advanced, climate-resilient and productive farming communities across South Africa.

Our Values

Affordability, sustainability, honesty and practical support for every farmer we work with.
